# Brag Plan: Ayodhya Agarbatti

## What is this app?
A premium e-commerce site selling hand-rolled sacred incense sticks made in Ayodhya from temple flowers, sandalwood, and essential oils — that also quietly sells arc-second-precision Vedic horoscope readings, whose "AI" branding turns out to stand for "Almighty Īśvara," not artificial intelligence.

## The angle
Play it as a quiet, premium incense-brand film — gold on deep maroon, temple smoke, the four fragrances — right up until the horoscope feature. The reveal that "AI" doesn't mean artificial intelligence, delivered with the exact same deadpan confidence as the rest of the copy, is the whole joke. No one winks. The brand doesn't know it's funny.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black-to-gold reveal: the gradient wordmark "Sacred Aroma" burns in over the ritual/temple-smoke background, same motion as the real hero. Immediate premium register — this reads like a luxury fragrance ad, not a joke video yet.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The four fragrances surface as real product cards: Espresso Ground, Madagascan Calm, Citrus Clarity, Creative Spark — each with its burn-time pill ("45-50 Minutes") and "33 Hand-Rolled Sticks."
- A fragrance pyramid detail (top / heart / base notes) pulls into frame, the kind of specific sourcing detail the site actually uses.
- Cut to the horoscope product card: "Janma Kundali · ₹99" with its real tagline about arc-second precision — introduced with total sincerity, same visual language as the incense cards.

## Outro / punchline
A single clarifying line lands, quoting the site's own words: "AI here means Almighty Īśvara — not artificial intelligence." Beat. Then the wordmark returns: "Ayodhya Agarbatti — Where prayer meets purity."

## User flow worth showing
Landing-page-led, with one real product-card flow: hero → browse the four incense fragrances (cards with burn time / stick count) → the horoscope section's own product card (Janma Kundali, ₹99, "Sign in" gated CTA). Not a multi-step app flow — the site's strongest material is these two adjacent, equally-serious product catalogs.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a quiet, premium incense-brand product film that holds its composure straight through one perfectly deadpan clarification about its "AI" feature
- Interpretation: slow, confident reveals (0.6-0.8s crossfades), generous letter-spacing, no bullet-point energy. The one joke gets zero extra emphasis — same pacing and restraint as everything around it — because playing it exactly as straight as the rest of the film is what makes it land.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #1A0505 body, radial gradient to #3E1414 / #0F0202 (deep sacred maroon)
- Accent: #D4AF37 (gold)
- Text: #FDFBF7 (ivory) on dark scenes; #1A1A1A (charcoal) on light/white product-card scenes
- Display font: Cinzel (headings), Playfair Display (serif accents), Dancing Script (italic flourish)
- Body font: Outfit
- Strongest visual element: the hero's gold gradient wordmark over the ritual-smoke background (`/images/ritual_bg.png`), and the white product cards with gold category pills / burn-time badges

## Share copy (draft)
We added an "AI" feature to our incense site. It stands for Almighty Īśvara. Not artificial intelligence. We were very clear about this.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with cinematic restraint
- Music: a slow, warm, temple-adjacent instrumental bed (soft strings/drone, no percussion drive) — low and unhurried
- Music treatment: starts under the hook at low volume, holds flat through the product beats, no swell until the outro where it lifts slightly and fades out under the final wordmark
- Music cue guidance: no bundled track chosen yet — detect cues at composition time; target one soft strong-cue at the horoscope card's arrival (~scene 3 start) and a gentle lift under the final wordmark; no beat-grid needed, nothing in this video reveals on a rhythm
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — restraint is the point
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft material-appropriate whoosh/shimmer on the wordmark reveal, a single quiet card-settle sound as the horoscope card arrives, nothing on the fragrance cards beyond a very light crossfade texture
- Audio-coupled moments: none required — no typing, no counters, no sequential list reveal fast enough to need sound sync
- Restraint rule: no comedic stingers, no joke "boing" or riser under the AI line — the punchline must be carried by the writing and the held silence, not by audio signaling "this is the funny part"

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 5s
Full-bleed ritual-smoke background (`ritual_bg.png` treatment: warm maroon, soft vignette). The gold gradient wordmark "Sacred Aroma" burns in with the same motion as the real hero (scale 1.1→1.0, opacity 0→1), tagline "Handcrafted in the Holy City" tracks in above it letter-spaced.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet, confident opening — the bed fades in under the wordmark, no attack
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: warm instrumental bed, very low
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The four fragrances — 6s
Four white product cards cross-fade/settle in (not simultaneously — a light stagger, ~0.25s apart, then all hold together): Espresso Ground, Madagascan Calm, Citrus Clarity, Creative Spark. Each shows its gold category pill, burn-time badge ("45-50 Minutes"), and "33 Hand-Rolled Sticks." Hold the full set 2s+ once settled so every name is readable.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the four cards stagger in left-to-right, then hold as a set
Audio intent: light, tactile card-arrival texture, still restrained
Audio-coupled idea: a very soft settle sound per card, quiet enough not to read as playful
Music: same bed, unchanged
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The horoscope card — 6s
Same white-card visual language. One card slides/settles into frame: "Janma Kundali · ₹99" with its real line about arc-second precision from the site. Beat of a full second on the settled card. Then, in the same typographic voice as the rest of the film (not a joke font), the line types/fades in below it: "AI here means Almighty Īśvara. Not artificial intelligence." Hold ~2s minimum so it can be read in full.
Sequential/interaction: none beyond the single card settle
Audio intent: the one quiet card-settle cue, then held silence under the clarifying line — no comedic accent
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: bed continues flat, no swell yet
Transition mood: slow crossfade (0.8s) → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
Return to the maroon ritual background. Wordmark "Ayodhya Agarbatti" settles center frame, small tagline beneath: "Where prayer meets purity." Music lifts slightly and fades under the hold.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: gentle resolution, warm fade-out
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: slight lift then fade to silence
Transition mood: — (final scene)

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic (restrained)
**Audio summary:** One continuous, unhurried instrumental bed under the whole film with no percussion drive or swell until the very end; two or three barely-there SFX (a shimmer on the wordmark, soft card-settle sounds) carry texture without ever signaling "joke incoming" — the deadpan clarification in Scene 3 is scored exactly like everything around it.
